I built an interactive simulator of global oil trade disruptions (closing the Strait of Hormuz and other chokepoints), based on my paper Supply Chain Networks (arXiv:2607.17491). It reached the front page of Hacker News (peaking at #3), with ~42,000 visitors running over 20,000 simulations in the first few days. Try it: [https://globaloilnetwork.staffinganalytics.io](https://globaloilnetwork.staffinganalytics.io)
