The interactive supply chain stress-testing simulator from my paper
[Supply Chain Networks (arXiv:2607.17491)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17491)
is now featured on
[UN Comtrade Labs](https://comtradeplus.un.org/Visualization/Labs), the
visualization gallery of the United Nations trade statistics platform, alongside
tools from the WTO. The listing describes it as "an interactive stress-testing
simulator of the global crude oil trade network."

Run it yourself: [https://globaloilnetwork.staffinganalytics.io](https://globaloilnetwork.staffinganalytics.io/?src=site)
