Hi, my name is Elioth, I'm a research scientist with a PhD in Operations Research from [Columbia University IEOR Department](https://ieor.columbia.edu/). Currently, I'm working on multiple projects applying Operations Research to solve real world problems with state-of-the-art solutions. I founded [Staffing Analytics](https://www.staffinganalytics.io/), created the retail analytics platform [Predictheus](https://predictheus.com/).

Currently, I serve as a Teaching Assistant Professor and Assistant Director for Industry Engagement in the DATA Department at [Lehigh University College of Business](https://business.lehigh.edu/), where I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in Supply Chain Management and Business Analytics, with a strong focus on creating practical learning experiences leveraging Generative AI. I have also served as an Adjunct Assistant Professor at Columbia University.

Before this, I served in the NYC Government as Assistant Commissioner of Operations Research and Chief Efficiency Officer for the [NYC Department of Correction](https://www.nyc.gov/site/doc/index.page) solving a lot of logistical optimization problems (including supply chain problems, transportation optimization, staff scheduling using Stochastic Discrete Optimization, and human tracking via Computer Vision). I also served as Deputy Director of Operations Research for the [Fire Department of New York City (FDNY)](https://www.nyc.gov/site/fdny/index.page), working on ambulance scheduling and call center staffing to minimize response times. This work was derived from my PhD thesis advised by Professors [David D. Yao](https://www.columbia.edu/~yao/) and [Henry Lam](https://www.columbia.edu/~khl2114/). 


While I have eclectic research interests spanning Applied Probability, Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Compressive Sensing, the common thread is a deep emphasis on technical correctness, practical applications, and maximizing the positive impact for society by applying Operations Research. My work has been recognized as a finalist in the INFORMS Doing Good with Good OR Paper Competition and the U.S. Army xTechLive Competition, and I am the author of the text *Supply Chain Analytics: A Data-driven Approach* (2026).
